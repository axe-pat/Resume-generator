## 4. Deal-Breakers

### Immigration Constraints (HARD REJECT — never overridden)
Reject roles in every lane that:
- Explicitly state "No CPT/OPT support"
- Require "US Citizen" or "Green Card holder"
- Require security clearance
- Require ITAR / US-person status
- Require permanent work authorization without sponsorship

Do not reject roles merely because they:
- Are silent on sponsorship (default: accept)
- Mention CPT/OPT/STEM OPT eligibility
- State that they do not sponsor H-1B or employment visas without separately excluding CPT/OPT/F-1

Lane B OPT treatment:
- "Will not sponsor H-1B" alone is a soft flag, not a hard reject; initial OPT does not require H-1B sponsorship
- Capture E-Verify status as yes / no / unknown where knowable
- E-Verify `no` or `unknown` is a flag for the STEM OPT extension horizon, not an automatic reject

> Immigration hard filters are absolute. No other signal — role quality, brand, fit score — can override a hard immigration reject.

### Role Type Mismatch (HARD REJECT)
Reject roles that are primarily:
- Software Engineering (Backend / Frontend / Full-stack)
- QA / SDET / Testing roles
- DevOps / SRE
- Data Engineering / Data Analyst (pure execution)
- IT / Support / Implementation roles

Exception: roles with clear product ownership or decision-making (e.g., Technical PM, Platform Product) are not rejected on title alone.

### Low Product Exposure (DEPRIORITIZE)
Deprioritize roles that:
- Are purely operational with no product or strategic decision-making
- Focus only on execution (reporting, coordination)
- Lack ownership or cross-functional influence

### Non-Tech / Low Innovation Environments (DEPRIORITIZE)
Deprioritize:
- Traditional non-tech industries with weak product culture
- Legacy environments with limited product innovation

Exception: digital/product transformation teams within traditional industries.

### Ambiguous / Low-Signal JDs (DEPRIORITIZE OR REJECT)
Reject or deprioritize roles that:
- Have vague or generic descriptions
- Do not mention product, users, metrics, or ownership
- Lack clarity on scope or impact
