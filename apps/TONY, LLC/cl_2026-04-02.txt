Dear Shop ONline New York Product Team,

During an enterprise trial at Hevo Data, a data engineering lead stopped the demo and asked one question: "If this pipeline breaks at 2am, how do I prove to my CFO what happened?" The engineering team had built something genuinely fast. But speed wasn't what was being evaluated. The lead needed a product that failed loudly enough to be auditable, not one that recovered quietly and left no record. That question reframed every product decision I made for the next year.

Job Monitoring had started as a debugging tool, something engineers used internally to trace errors. I recognized it as the artifact enterprise evaluators actually needed to feel safe before signing. I repositioned it as a first-class audit surface, wrote the user stories that scoped it as a product rather than a utility, and worked with engineering and customer success to shape it into what showed up in every subsequent enterprise trial. Visibility is a product decision. That repositioning reduced mean time to resolution by roughly 40% and turned trials that had previously stalled into signed contracts. The feature that closes enterprise deals isn't always on the roadmap. Sometimes it's already built, just scoped wrong.

That same pattern shows up in how I think about e-commerce. The hardest drop-off in a shopping funnel is rarely at checkout. It lives earlier, in the moment a user can't find what they came for or encounters friction they can't name. The gap between what users say they want and what the session data shows them doing is exactly where a sharp backlog and well-scoped user stories create compounding value. That is the gap I want to work on at Shop ONline New York.

In the first 90 days, I would map the end-to-end user journey from discovery through checkout, identify the highest-friction drop-off point using session data or direct user interviews, and bring back a prioritized backlog of two or three targeted improvements with success metrics attached. The open question I'd want answered on day one: where in that journey do users hesitate longest, and does the current product surface know?

Sincerely,
