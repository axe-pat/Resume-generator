Product Owner Intern

About the job
Non-Paid Internship for Product Owner
Company: Shop ONline New York
Location: Fully Remote
Duration: 3 months
Number of Hours Per Week: 20

About Shop ONline New York: 
Shop ONline New York is at the forefront of e-commerce innovation, striving to deliver exceptional shopping experiences through user-centric design and strategic product development. We are looking for a passionate Product Owner Intern to join our team and help bring our vision to life. This role offers a unique opportunity to work on exciting projects and lead them to successful completion.

Role Overview: 
As a Product Owner Intern, you will play a pivotal role in the development and implementation of our products. You will work closely with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to ensure that our product vision is translated into reality. You will be responsible for managing the product backlog, defining user stories, and ensuring seamless communication between teams.

Key Responsibilities:
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand their needs and translate them into product requirements.
- Develop and prioritize the product backlog, ensuring it aligns with the overall business strategy.
- Work closely with design, development, and marketing teams to ensure successful product delivery.
- Lead the product development process, from concept to launch, ensuring that the project stays on track.
- Facilitate communication and collaboration between teams to ensure alignment and transparency.
- Conduct user research and gather feedback to continuously improve the product.
- Ensure that the product meets the highest standards of quality and delivers value to users.

Qualifications:

- Strong understanding of product development processes and agile methodologies
.- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
.- Ability to manage multiple priorities and projects simultaneously
.- Analytical mindset with the ability to translate complex requirements into actionable tasks
.- Familiarity with project management tools such as Jira or Trello
.- A proactive approach to problem-solving and continuous improvement
.- Previous experience in a product management or similar role is a plus but not required
.

Exceptional Internship Benefits:

- Gain hands-on experience as a Product Owner within a leading e-commerce company
.- Develop your skills in product management, user research, and agile methodologies
.- Work closely with experienced professionals and receive mentorship and guidance
.- Enhance your resume with practical experience in a high-demand field
.- Build a professional network within the tech and e-commerce industries
.

Application Process: If you are passionate about product development and eager to enhance your skills as a Product Owner, please apply for this exciting internship opportunity.
