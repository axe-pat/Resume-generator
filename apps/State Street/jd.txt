OTC Alpha Product,6 Months Full-time Internship (Jul- Dec 2026) 

About the job
Role Overview

The OTC Alpha Product Intern will support the product leadership and delivery teams by helping manage the pipeline of work across the OTC Alpha product team. This role is ideal for a highly organized, detail oriented intern who wants exposure to enterprise product delivery, complex financial services, and cross functional execution.

The intern will play a key role in tracking work, improving transparency, strengthening reporting, and helping the team tighten execution and deliverables across multiple initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

Work Pipeline & Delivery Management

 Maintain and update the team’s pipeline of work, ensuring priorities, dependencies, and delivery status are clearly reflected
 Help track commitments, milestones, and deliverables across product and delivery workstreams
 Support follow ups on open items to improve delivery discipline and execution clarity


Jira & Workflow Support

 Learn and actively manage Jira tickets, epics, and boards used by the OTC Alpha team
 Ensure Jira items are accurate, up to date, well described, and aligned to agreed outcomes
 Assist with backlog hygiene, status updates, and alignment between planned vs. delivered work


Reporting & Transparency

 Help create and maintain clear, concise reporting for product leadership
 Support development of dashboards and summaries that highlight progress, risks, and bottlenecks
 Identify opportunities to improve how work is tracked, reported, and communicated


Execution & Quality Support

 Assist the team in tightening deliverables, including clarity of scope, acceptance criteria, and outcomes
 Help identify gaps, inconsistencies, or risks in execution and flag them early
 Support preparation of materials for internal updates, reviews, or leadership read outs


What You’ll Learn

 How large scale enterprise product teams manage delivery across multiple initiatives
 How OTC derivatives and complex instruments are supported within a modern financial platform
 How tools like Jira and reporting dashboards are used to drive execution and accountability
 How product, technology, operations, and client teams work together in a regulated environment


Qualifications & Skills

 Currently pursuing a degree in Business, Finance, Technology, Operations, or a related field
 Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
 Comfortable working with structured tools (Jira, spreadsheets, dashboards)
 Clear written and verbal communication skills
 Interest in financial services, product management, or program delivery
 Ability to manage multiple tasks and follow through on commitments


Nice to Have

 Exposure to Jira, Agile delivery, or project management tools
 Interest in fintech, capital markets, or enterprise platforms
 Strong analytical or reporting mindset
