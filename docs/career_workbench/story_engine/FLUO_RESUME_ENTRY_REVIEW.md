## Promotion checkpoint

Move Fluo into Experience only when at least one of these is true:

- a written role/title is established;
- the dependency product is launched to real users with attributable evidence;
- an institutional pilot or partnership is actually confirmed;
- the engagement produces two distinct, defensible shipped outcomes that warrant
