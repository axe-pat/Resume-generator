# Resume relaunch — August 20, 2026

## Outcome

The relaunch now uses a hybrid workflow:

- repeatable PM generation for role strategy and story selection;
- a factual review layer that rejects unsupported rewrites even when the model score rises;
- a compact, role-selected Fluo entry under `PROJECTS & CONSULTING`;
- a separate Lane C document path for campus roles and required cover letters;
- final DOCX render review before delivery.

The first five application resumes and the required Viterbi cover letter were built
and rendered as one-page documents.

## What failed in the prior cycle

The archive shows four recurring problems:

1. **Identity mismatch.** The first visible roles were all software-engineering titles,
   while several bullets and summaries claimed PM-scale ownership. A recruiter could
   reasonably read the profile as an engineer trying to narrate product work.
2. **Attribution risk.** Several older resumes used platform-scale revenue, adoption,
   or business outcomes as if they were individually caused. Even when the underlying
   project was real, the ownership line was not always clear.
3. **Density.** One-page output often meant eleven long experience bullets, a summary,
   education detail, and six skill rows. Passing a page-fit check did not make the page
   fast to read.
4. **Wrong optimization target.** An 8.0–9.0 internal writing score measured sentence
   quality and JD resemblance. It did not test eligibility, ten-second identity,
   factual defensibility, or whether the strongest proof appeared first.

The strongest external resume examples point toward a simpler pattern: one page,
standard ATS structure, short role blocks, the most bullets on recent work, JD language
only where genuine, and no need for a summary when the experience itself supplies the
identity. References: [Go Apply PM resume case study](https://goapply.substack.com/p/the-resume-that-got-me-pm-interviews)
and [Exponent PM resume guide](https://www.tryexponent.com/blog/how-to-write-the-perfect-product-manager-resume).

## New positioning policy

### FlairX

Use as the first full Experience entry:

`FlairX AI | AI Product Manager Intern | Jun 2026 – Aug 2026 | Remote`

The FlairX deck and story document are the evidence sources. Draft gold-reference
metrics remain excluded unless independently confirmed.

### Fluo

Use now, but under Projects & Consulting:

`FLUO | Product Strategy & Build, Founder Engagement | Jul 2026 – Present`

Select one bullet by role. Do not call this employment or use a formal consultant/PM
title until the founder confirms it. Promotion to Experience requires a formal role,
a real-user launch, a confirmed institutional pilot, or a second shipped outcome.

### Gojek and Hevo titles

Keep the official titles visible:

- `Senior Software Engineer | Product & Marketplace Scope`
- `Software Engineer 2 | Data Platform & Product Scope`

The descriptor names the functional scope; it does not replace the employment title.
Do not change these to Product Manager or Technical Product Manager without written
employer/title support.

## QA gate for future resumes

A resume is send-ready only if all of these are true:

- the role is actually eligible before tailoring effort;
- the first ten seconds produce the intended identity;
- official titles remain visible;
- each metric and ownership verb can survive an interview drill-down;
- no current project assumption is rewritten as a realized outcome;
- the page renders cleanly at readable size;
- model score is treated as a diagnostic, not an approval.
