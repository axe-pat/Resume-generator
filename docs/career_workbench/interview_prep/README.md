# Interview Prep

Use this area for company-specific prep notes, TMAY drafts, recruiter-call talking points, HireVue answers, and interview debriefs.

## Fast Workflow

1. Copy `prep_template.md` into `private/active/YYYY-MM-DD_Company_Role/`.
2. Add the JD link, artifacts, and role thesis.
3. Pull 3-5 reusable stories from `story_sources/README.md`.
4. Draft the company-specific TMAY and question bank.
5. After the interview, add a short debrief.
6. Move the folder from `active/` to `archive/`.

## Placement Rule

Interview prep folders are the **consumer** of stories. They reference `story_engine/`, `story_sources/`, and `STORY_BANK_RICH.md`; they do not become a second story bank. Company-specific positioning that should survive across chats can also live as a Cursor rule under `ResumeGenerator v1/.cursor/rules/` (see `sweetgreen-ops.mdc`).

## Why Private Is Ignored

Prep folders often contain rough notes, copied JDs, binary docs, and personal interview reflections. They are useful inside the project but do not need to be committed. The durable learnings should be distilled into tracked source files once they become reusable.
