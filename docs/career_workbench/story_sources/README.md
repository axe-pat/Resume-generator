# Story Sources

This file is the human-readable index for reusable stories and answer material. It does not replace the prompt-facing story bank; it points you to the source of truth and helps you find the right story quickly.

## Durable Story Sources

- `cover_letters/story_bank/Behaviourals.md`
  Rough behavioral prep, TMAY drafts, and company-specific PM positioning notes. Good for finding old answer language.

- `cover_letters/story_bank/Day 6*December 16.md`
  Longer behavioral story bank with TMAY variants and main behavioral stories.

- `cover_letters/story_bank/Hevo 2.0 revised Product story.md`
  Deep Hevo product/story material. Best source for enterprise pivot, segmentation, market/competitor analysis, and product judgment.

- `docs/reference/STORY_BANK_RICH.md`
  Weak-variant reference and high-signal story material used to audit/rescue resume and cover-letter variants.

- `docs/career_workbench/story_sources/interview_story_scripts.md`
  Polished spoken versions of reusable stories. Best for interview prep, TMAY/Why Company drafts, and behavioral/product answer scripts.

- `resume/freeform/prompts/freeform_master_v2.txt`
  PM-track resume bullet source. Use this when a story is ready to become resume-grade.

- `resume/freeform/prompts/freeform_master_nonpm.txt`
  Non-PM/strategy/business-ops track source. Use this for venture-builder, strategy, operations, and commercial framing.

## Promotion Rule

When interview prep creates a stronger story, do not paste the raw notes directly into resume prompts. First distill it into:

- stable facts
- one clean STAR version
- 2-3 company/role angles
- metrics and ownership boundaries
- phrases to avoid or use carefully

Then update the relevant durable source.
