  S/D/F/X scores (stakes / difficulty / defensibility / distinctiveness).

- **HOLD FOR WORDING DECISION:** keep for fixture reproduction; do not select.
- **FIXTURE-ONLY:** preserve the document but do not admit as a selectable
  variant.

## Critical review items

1. **Amazon latency wording.** The submitted gold says `Traded ±4% fare-quote
   accuracy...`; StudyFetch later uses the clearer `Traded live fare
   recalculation... held fare variance within 4%...`. Both are preserved as
   separate reviewed variants because that is what the final artifacts contain.
   Decide later whether the StudyFetch wording should supersede the Amazon
   sibling; this step does not silently rewrite history.

2. **Amazon heading mismatch.** The gold says `SKILLS & INTERESTS` but contains no
   Interests row. The fixture preserves the artifact exactly. Treat this as an
   expected incumbent defect (`SKILLS_HEADING_INACCURATE` is a v2 release
   blocker), not a pattern future resumes must copy.

3. **Amazon cross-render page count.** The submitted Microsoft Word PDF is one
   page. The same DOCX currently moves only the Community row to page 2 under
   bundled LibreOffice. The fixture therefore treats the DOCX as text authority
   and the submitted PDF as visual authority. A portable one-page render should
   be a challenger improvement, not grounds to mutate approved content.

4. **StudyFetch Community punctuation.** The final v4 Niveda row has no terminal
   period. The registry preserves this exact text. The assembly layer may add
   uniform terminal punctuation mechanically at render time, but the gold
   source should remain exact.

5. **Metadata is reviewed judgment, not extraction.** Exact wording, section,
   employer/project, and source hashes are documentary. Archetype, value
   signals, outcome tier, and line cost are selection metadata. The most
   consequential calls are:
   - Recruiting Engine outcomes = `observed-behavior`, not throughput;
   - the wrong-match recovery = `decision-or-organization`, not a weak bug fix;
   - Fluo field validation = a diagnostic closed evidence loop;
   - line cost is an observed fixture estimate, not a universal renderer promise.

## What this permanently captures

- One bullet makes one argument; its mechanism and outcome close that argument.
- Prefer the strongest available user/business or behavioral outcome over input
  volume and build counts.
- Internal names and locations require functional context.
- Supporting projects can displace lower-marginal corporate bullets when they
  are materially better proof of the JD's top criterion.
- Project proof is explicit and bounded, not a new role family.
- Final-document wording, not an earlier payload, is the canonical source.
