AKSHAT PATHAK — MATERIAL VARIANT CHALLENGER
============================================

You are auditing one complete resume story family. Your job is not to polish
every incumbent sentence. Your job is to recover the smallest set of materially
different, exceptional variants that the evidence can support.

Inputs:

STORY ID
{{STORY_ID}}

STORY EVIDENCE
{{STORY_EVIDENCE}}

CURRENT SELECTABLE VARIANTS
{{INCUMBENT_VARIANTS}}

KNOWN GOLD OR REVIEWED SIBLINGS
{{KNOWN_GOLD_VARIANTS}}

CRITERIA THIS STORY MAY NEED TO PROVE
{{CRITERION_CATALOG}}

PAGE / PROFILE CONTEXTS
{{PROFILE_CONTEXT}}

CORE METHOD — CLAIM SPINE BEFORE WORDING

1. Treat the story as an evidence graph, not as a sentence.
2. Enumerate no more than four genuinely distinct claim spines:
   trigger or observation -> judgment -> decision or artifact -> attributable consequence.
3. State the single hiring question each spine proves. Topical relevance is not
   enough; the evidence must directly answer that question.
4. Lead with the scarcest causal atom: the insight, trade-off, shipped artifact,
   ownership decision, or directly attributable result another candidate could
   least easily reproduce.
5. Use one decision or artifact and one earned detail. Do not inventory features,
   tools, workflow stages, or every fact available.
6. End on the strongest attributable consequence on that same causal path.
   Prefer user/business behavior, then observed behavior, then a real decision or
   organizational change, then quality/efficiency. Activity volume, architecture
   size, and build counts are context, not outcomes, when a downstream result exists.
7. Explicitly exclude attractive adjacent facts that belong to a different path.
   Shared topic does not create causality.
8. Compress by information role, not arbitrary character count. Protect the scarce
   atom, decision, earned detail, and matched consequence. Remove adjacent outcomes,
   architecture inventory, internal shorthand, and secondary currencies first.

CRITICAL VETOES — A VARIANT FAILS IF ANY ANSWER IS NO

- Is the underlying work material enough to deserve space on this resume?
- Do all causal edges belong to this one story path?
- Is Akshat's judgment or counterfactual ownership visible?
- Does the decision/artifact answer the trigger or observation?
- Does the consequence close the exact claim made at the start?
- Can an outside recruiter understand what changed in one read?
- Are all facts and numbers present in STORY EVIDENCE or an incumbent/reviewed
  sibling? If not, do not write them.

NON-REGRESSION COMPARISON

- Compare within each hiring question, not by one averaged score.
- The incumbent wins a tie.
- A challenger replaces an incumbent only when every critical veto passes, it
  creates at least one material improvement, and it causes no material loss in
  stakes, causal integrity, ownership, outcome quality, outsider legibility, or
  page-relative signal value.
- Cleaner wording alone is not a material win.
- A shorter variant wins only after material value is tied.
- Keep two siblings only when they prove different hiring questions. The same
  evidence reordered under a different opener is not a distinct variant.
- A known gold sibling is a strong incumbent, not an untouchable answer. Challenge
  it honestly and retain it unchanged when no candidate is materially better.
- Never merge two partial variants to manufacture one impressive causal chain.

EXHAUSTIVE RULEBOOK CARD — REQUIRED FOR EVERY CHALLENGER

Do not sample whichever rules feel relevant. Complete all fifteen dimensions
below for every candidate. Each dimension consolidates a named set of canonical
rules; the checked-in coverage catalog maps every rule to exactly one owner.
Use `not_applicable` only when the rule genuinely cannot apply to this story
type, and explain why. A candidate marked `accept_challenger` cannot have a
failed dimension.

- materiality
- criterion_and_scarce_signal
- archetype_fit
- single_story_spine
- causal_closure
- counterfactual_ownership
- mechanism_specificity
- earned_detail
- attribution_and_outcome
- outsider_legibility
- cognitive_load
- evidence_loop
- rhetorical_integrity
- fact_containment
- family_non_regression

After completing that semantic card, deterministic admission code separately
checks the actual text for weak/forbidden openers, subordinate openers, known
generic mechanisms, em dashes, parentheses, personal pronouns, forbidden filler,
decorative metric openers, vague stakeholder nouns, fragment-list outcomes,
trade-off wording, punctuation, and readability/cohesion proxies. Do not claim a
proxy proves causal coherence; it only forces closer review.

RENDERING RULE

Render each surviving challenger as:

  [scarcest causal atom] + [one decision/artifact with one earned detail]
  + [strongest causally matched consequence]

Use the canonical variant rulebook for punctuation, attribution, readability,
archetype labels, and resume-level constraints. Those rules are necessary but not
sufficient; checklist compliance is not evidence of material quality.

Return only valid JSON. Do not use markdown fences.
