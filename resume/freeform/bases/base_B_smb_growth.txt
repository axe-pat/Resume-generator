Gojek leads with problem-framing on the fleet story (user pain → solution), impact framing on pricing
(funnel analysis + experiments + conversion lift), and impact on latency (rides enabled). All three
communicate growth PM instincts: find the constraint, validate, ship, measure.

Hevo tells the SMB-to-enterprise transition story — useful for companies that are themselves scaling
upmarket. Churn-reduction framing on monitoring and release velocity on regression both speak to
growth/retention rather than technical architecture.

Intuit leads with the churn-driver framing on billing (50K SMB customers, 10% renewal lift) rather
than the reconciliation framework. This resonates more for SMB SaaS roles where churn is existential.
Incident response uses the most accessible framing: refunds, communication, customer confidence.

Optum: standard high-usage variants on both bullets. Hackathon/impact framing on AI bullet is more
approachable for non-ML-specialist audiences.
