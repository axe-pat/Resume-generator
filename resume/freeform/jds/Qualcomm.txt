Role Overview

As a Qualcomm Product Management intern, you will assist in business operations by gathering and analyzing data, generating reports, and presenting findings to senior management. Support in business development and partnerships across the ecosystem, using strong verbal and written communication skills to convey complex information to diverse audiences. Additionally, you will review customer requirements, identify gaps in Qualcomm’s offerings, and exercise creativity and problem-solving skills to innovate and suggest new products or processes, often dealing with incomplete or conflicting information.

This internship supports the Product Management track. Based on your resume, you will be aligned to projects within this track.

Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s, master’s, or Ph.D. degree program in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Business, or a related field.
Must be available for 11–14 weeks during Summer 2026 (May–September).

1+ years of academic or project experience in:

Qualcomm’s Internship Program
