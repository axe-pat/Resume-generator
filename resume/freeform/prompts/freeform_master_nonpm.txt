FOUR-ARCHETYPE SYSTEM — DISTRIBUTION ACROSS 11 BULLETS:

  Each bullet must be classified as one of four archetypes at generation time:

  DIAGNOSTIC    — insight-first; opener encodes a detected problem, gap, or reframing
                  Verbs: Identified, Diagnosed, Recognized, Surfaced, Found, Caught,
                         Reframed, Synthesized, Evaluated, Assessed, Mapped
                  NOTE: "Caught" = DIAGNOSTIC (detection verb), not action-first.

  ACTION-FIRST  — execution-first; opener names the delivered action, operating move,
                  implementation step, or owned artifact
                  Verbs: Built, Delivered, Established, Improved, Reduced, Accelerated,
                         Designed, Guided, Implemented, Stabilized, Integrated, Contained

  CONTEXT-FIRST — scope/goal-first; opener states the operating scope, rollout frame,
                  or scale context before the mechanism follows
                  Pattern: "Expanded [goal] by [method]..." / "Stabilized [surface] by..."
                  These are NOT wrong just because they lack a corrected assumption.

  IMPACT-FIRST  — outcome-first; opener leads with a credentialing metric or win
                  Pattern: "Won...", "Generated $X...", "Unlocked access for [N]..."
                  Use sparingly; only when the result is itself the strongest signal.

  Target distribution by route:

    Archetype        Strategy/BizOps   Research/Intel   Client/Impl   Ops/Exec

    Diagnostic           4–5               5–6             3–4          3–4

    Context-first        1–2                 1             1–2          1–2
    Impact-first           1                 1               1            1
