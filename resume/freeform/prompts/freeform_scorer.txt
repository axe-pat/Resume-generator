SCORING RUBRIC — per bullet (1–10 scale)

IMPORTANT — FOUR ARCHETYPES, FOUR SCORING TRACKS:
This rubric covers four recognized archetypes. Each can score 10 when executed
correctly against its own criteria. Do NOT apply Diagnostic criteria to
Context-first bullets, or Action-first criteria to Impact-first bullets.
The 1-10 scale below defines score levels; the ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC 10/9/8 CRITERIA
section below defines what "precisely executed" means per archetype.

No archetype has an imposed ceiling. A perfect Context-first scores 10 as readily
as a perfect Diagnostic. If execution/platform stories consistently score lower,
that is a signal the mechanism or causal chain needs work — not that the archetype
is inferior.

SHARED SCALE (1–10) — apply after archetype-specific evaluation:

      Causal chain complete with no invisible bridging step. Register clean: no em
      dashes, no forbidden words, no "and...and" chain (max 1 "and" per bullet),
      no late-arriving subject. Could not have been written by anyone else.

9   — As above, with one minor gap: opener slightly broad for the archetype, OR
      earned detail passes detection but removal test is marginal (still a good

      require re-reading. Clearly above average.

8   — Correct or defensible archetype. Mechanism mostly visible. Metric does real
      work (not decorating). Earned detail present but only passes detection (fails
      removal — bullet is still strong without it). A real PM wrote this; competent

7   — Reasonable archetype but partial execution. Mechanism partially visible (what
      is clear; how is vague or implied). Metric present but not fully earning its

6   — Opener generic or archetype misapplied. Mechanism absent or very vague.

5   — Opener borderline or weakly chosen. No visible mechanism. No earned detail.

4   — Scope-claiming opener without mechanism (e.g. "Owned X from definition to

3   — Uses a FORBIDDEN OPENER PATTERN. Mechanism absent. Generic outcome.
      Actively hurts the resume.

2   — Forbidden opener + vague outcome + no metric doing work.

1   — All failures: forbidden opener, no mechanism, no earned detail, metric as

DIAGNOSTIC (insight-first: Identified / Diagnosed / Recognized / Surfaced /

      it). Mechanism visible. Metric does real work. No forced contrast.

ACTION-FIRST (execution-first: Built / Shipped / Designed / Launched / Drove /
              Standardized / Introduced / Cut / Reshaped / Converted / Led /

  9:  Method specific, causal chain mostly complete, one minor bridging step
      implied but inferable. Earned detail passes detection.
  8:  Method specific but causal chain has one invisible step that requires
      inference. OR earned detail present but fails removal.

CONTEXT-FIRST (scope/goal-first: "Expanded [goal] by [method]..." /
               "Serving [scale]..." / goal-scope opener that orients before acting)
  10: Scope/goal opener names something specific enough that a non-participant
      could not reconstruct it exactly (geography, partner type, operating
      constraint). Mechanism in second clause names a concrete design decision or
      deliverable — not a list of activities. Causal bridge between mechanism and
      outcome is EXPLICIT — the intermediate step connecting what was done to the
      result appears in the bullet (e.g. "by onboarding fleet operators" bridges
      "designed API contract" to "18% supply growth"). Outcome proportionate.
  9:  Scope specific, mechanism concrete, causal bridge present but slightly thin.
      OR one minor register issue.
  8:  Scope specific. Mechanism names the artifact but causal bridge is implied
      rather than explicit. OR mechanism is an activity list rather than a specific
      design decision.
  NOTE: Context-first is the correct archetype for execution/platform stories
  where the goal is self-evident and there is no genuine corrected assumption.
  Do NOT force Diagnostic on these stories. Do NOT penalize Context-first for
  lacking a diagnostic insight — that would be WRONG_ARCHETYPE, not WEAK_MECHANISM.

IMPACT-FIRST (outcome-first: "Won..." / "Generated $X..." / "Restored [X] for [N]...")
  10: Outcome is directly attributable to Akshat's specific work — not a shared

  9:  Attribution solid, mechanism present but secondary. One minor gap in the

  8:  Outcome credentialing, attribution defensible, but mechanism is thin (stated

  NOTE: Impact-first hard cap = 2 per resume. Only for competition wins, isolated
  revenue figures, or concrete human scale where Akshat's contribution is isolable.

ARCHETYPE EVALUATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For each bullet, identify the archetype used, then assess correctness.

IDENTIFYING ARCHETYPE FROM THE OPENER:
  Diagnostic:       Starts with an insight verb: Identified / Diagnosed / Recognized /
                    Surfaced / Found / Caught / Reframed / Synthesized [data → insight]
                    + a non-obvious finding or corrected assumption.
                    NOTE: "Caught" is DIAGNOSTIC even though it has action energy —
                    it encodes a detection insight, not an execution step.
  Action-first:     Starts with an execution verb naming a specific deliverable or
                    method: Built / Shipped / Designed / Launched / Drove /
                    Standardized / Introduced / Cut / Reshaped / Converted / Led /
                    Prototyped / Halted / Prioritized / Generated / Expanded.
                    `Led` is permitted when the object itself is concrete and
                    credentialing, such as a named product/workstream or recovery
                    after a specific failure. Do NOT treat every `Led ...` opener
                    as forbidden. Only the generic patterns named below are banned.
  Context-first:    Starts with a scope/goal statement that orients the reader before
                    moving to mechanism: "Expanded [goal] by [method]..." or a
                    scale/context framing like "Serving Fortune 500 data teams..."
                    The opener answers WHAT we were solving and at what scale.
                    Distinct from Diagnostic: no corrected assumption leads; the
                    goal may be self-evident. Distinct from Action-first: opener
                    names the goal, not the method.
  Impact-first:     Starts with a metric or credentialing achievement that stands
                    alone: "Won Optum's global hackathon...", "Generated $3.2M..."
                    Hard cap: 2 per resume.
  Forbidden:        Starts with a pattern from the forbidden opener list.

ARCHETYPE CORRECTNESS CHECKS:
  Diagnostic is WRONG when: the "insight" restates what was done rather than
    reframing what the problem was. Test: remove the insight phrase — does the
    bullet collapse into an indistinguishable execution story? If no (the action
    and metric stand alone without the insight), the diagnostic opener is forced.

  Mechanism-first is WRONG when: the opener names a generic activity rather than
    a specific credentialing method. "Conducted analysis," "performed research,"
    "led reviews" are activities, not mechanisms. The method must be specific
    enough that a non-participant would not have guessed it (e.g., "behavioral
    analysis and competitive benchmarking across booking funnel stages").

  Impact-first is WRONG when: the leading metric is a shared platform number
    or team-level result where Akshat's individual contribution is not isolable,
    OR when the insight is actually the primary value signal (using impact-first
    loses the PM differentiation).

  Context-first is WRONG when: the scope/goal opener is so generic that any PM
    could have written it ("Expanded operations by...") without the specificity of
    geography, partner type, or operating constraint that makes it credentialing.
    It is also WRONG when the story has a genuine corrected assumption — in that
    case Diagnostic is the stronger archetype.
    It is NOT wrong simply because there is no diagnostic insight. Execution and
    platform stories legitimately belong here. Do NOT assign WRONG_ARCHETYPE to
    a Context-first bullet just because it lacks a corrected assumption — that is
    the point of Context-first.

If the archetype is WRONG, assign failure_mode = WRONG_ARCHETYPE and deduct
2 points from what the bullet would otherwise score (minimum score of 2).

EARNED DETAIL EVALUATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every bullet should contain one phrase that only someone who was in that situation
would write. Evaluate with two sequential tests:

DETECTION TEST: Could someone who was not in the room write a different, equally
  specific phrase — and be wrong? If no, the phrase is not earned.
  (Generic specificity: "18% supply increase." Earned specificity: "had been
  capping conversion" — this encodes the team's prior misattribution.)

REMOVAL TEST: If the earned detail is removed entirely, does the bullet lose
  specificity or credibility? If no, the detail is decorative — it passes
  detection but does no meaningful work. Flag as DECORATIVE_DETAIL.
  Note: a phrase can be specific and still be removable. This test catches that.

Scoring impact:
  Passes both tests:   contributes to 9–10 range
  Passes detection only (fails removal): contributes to 8 range; note the gap
  Fails detection:     contributes to 6–7 range at best (TEMPLATE_FEEL likely)

Contrast test (for "not X but Y" phrases): Does this contrast reflect a belief
  the team was genuinely operating under, that this work overturned? If the
  contrast is logically entailed or tautological, it fails detection entirely.
  SECTION-LEVEL CAP: Only one "not X but Y" or "not X" phrase is allowed across
  all {{BULLET_COUNT}} bullets. A second instance, even if individually valid, should be flagged
  as FORCED_CONTRAST.

CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION CHECK
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Run this check on any bullet that cites a large-scale metric (company-wide
revenue, platform-level scale such as "50M members" or "$110M+ revenue"):

ATTRIBUTION TEST: Does the mechanism described plausibly and directly produce
  the cited metric through Akshat's specific actions?

  PASS: The mechanism directly causes the metric. Example: "launching the
    supply platform and onboarding external fleet partners" → "18% more supply
    → $110M+ annual platform revenue." Akshat's work is the primary lever and
    the causal chain is bridgeable at the stated scale.

  FAIL — assign failure_mode = ATTRIBUTION_MISMATCH if any of:
    (a) Platform-level metric, individual mechanism: metric is company-wide
        or team-owned; Akshat's action is one of many contributing inputs.
        The bullet cannot pass Impact-first attribution.
        Example: "Enabled $20M+ annual revenue by integrating one provider"
        — the revenue belongs to the full care network, not one integration.
    (b) Scale mismatch: the mechanism at the described scope cannot
        plausibly produce a metric 10x or more larger.
    (c) Causal chain invisible: the bullet cites the metric but omits the
        step connecting the action to the outcome, leaving the reader to
        assume the link.

  Fix guidance (put in note): specify which metric fails and recommend the
  correct framing — typically switching from Impact-first to Mechanism-first
  or Diagnostic, keeping the metric as supporting context rather than the
  leading outcome. Deduct 1 point from the bullet's otherwise applicable score.
