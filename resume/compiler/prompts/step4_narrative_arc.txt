RESUME COMPILER — STEP 4: NARRATIVE ARC CHECK
=============================================
Version 1.0 | Spec ref: Section 7

You are reviewing a set of resume bullets that the compiler has already selected
via deterministic rules. Your job is NOT to re-score stories or re-apply
compiler logic. Your only job is to check whether the 3 selected stories for
each company block form a coherent narrative arc — and if not, to propose a
minimal fix.

Read the selected stories below, then produce a structured review in the format
specified at the end of this prompt.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT A GOOD NARRATIVE ARC LOOKS LIKE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A good arc means: the three bullets inside a company block read as a coherent
chapter of work. Each story connects to or builds on the others. A reader can
construct a single sentence about your role at that company.

  GOOD (Hevo Data canonical arc):
  • "Identified the enterprise gap and made the strategic pivot"
  • "Drove the core technical bet that made the pivot possible"
  • "Built the surface that proved reliability to customers"
  → These build on each other. There is a clear connective thread.

  BAD (three independent stories):
  • "Strategic pivot to enterprise"
  • "Query engine performance improvement"
  • "Release velocity framework"
  → These are three unrelated initiatives. No connective thread. A reader
    cannot construct a narrative about what you did at Hevo.

A block does NOT need to tell a linear story. Parallel efforts that share a
theme (e.g. "three things that proved enterprise readiness") are coherent.
Three completely unrelated initiatives are incoherent.

YOUR TASK
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For each company block (Gojek, Hevo Data, Intuit, Optum), do the following:

1. ASSESS: Does this block form a coherent narrative arc?
   - "coherent" = the three stories connect in theme, trajectory, or outcome
   - "incoherent" = three isolated initiatives with no connective thread

2. DIAGNOSE (only if incoherent): Which story breaks the arc? Name the story_id.

3. PROPOSE (only if incoherent): Name one substitute story_id from the same
   company's pool that would improve arc coherence. Choose from the available
   stories listed below.

4. EXPLAIN: One sentence on why the arc works or why the substitute improves it.

Available story pool (all experience-section stories by company):
  Gojek:     gojek_external_fleet, gojek_pricing_tier, gojek_quote_latency
  Hevo Data: hevo_enterprise_shift, hevo_monitoring, hevo_query_engine,
             hevo_regression, hevo_genai
  Intuit:    intuit_billing, intuit_prioritization, intuit_incident,
             intuit_roadmap
  Optum:     optum_ai_affordability, optum_provider, optum_backend

Rules:
- Only substitute a story from the SAME company's pool. Never reassign to a
  different company.
- Do not force an arc that isn't real. If the company's pool genuinely has no
  connective stories, accept the weaker arc and say so.
- Do not re-run scoring rules or question why the compiler chose these stories.
  Work with what was selected.
- Optum always has 1 bullet. There is no arc to assess for a single bullet.
  Mark Optum as "N/A — single bullet block."

PROTECTED STORIES — these story_ids must NEVER be flagged as "breaking story"
and must NEVER be proposed for removal, regardless of narrative arc reasoning:

  intuit_incident
    WHY: Cross-functional incident leadership (crisis response, coordination
    under pressure, direct customer impact) is an intentionally distinct PM
    competency from billing accuracy and prioritization framework work. These
    three stories do NOT need to form a thematic arc — they each demonstrate
    a different core PM skill (business impact / process ownership / execution
    under pressure). This variety is a feature, not a weakness. The incident
    story is the most-used Intuit bullet in handcrafted resumes (20× usage)
    and must be preserved unless the JD has zero operational or execution
    signals. Accept any Intuit block containing intuit_incident as "coherent"
    unless the other two stories are also clearly misaligned with each other.

  gojek_external_fleet, gojek_pricing_tier, gojek_quote_latency
    WHY: Gojek only has three stories in the pool — there is no substitute.
    Never flag Gojek as "incoherent" when all three stories are already
    selected; accept the arc as is.

IMPORTANT: If your arc reasoning would lead you to propose removing a protected
story, override that reasoning. Instead, mark the block as "coherent" and note
in the Explanation that the variety is intentional.
