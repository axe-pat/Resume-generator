# Behaviourals

## What are the *essential* skills of a PM at Epson?

### 1. **Lifecycle & Foresight Thinking**

> You must get decisions right before launch.
> 

**Example:**

- A receipt printer model ships once every few years
- You can’t add a fan later if thermal issues show up in kitchens
- A projector lens decision affects cost, brightness, and venue fit for 5+ years

**Translation:** PM thinks *years ahead*, not sprints ahead.

---

### 2. **Reliability & Failure-Impact Awareness**

> Failure is visible, stressful, and costly.
> 

**Example:**

- Kitchen printer fails during dinner rush → orders lost, staff panic
- Large venue projector glitches mid-event → brand damage, refunds
- POS printer jams → checkout lines halt

**Translation:** PM designs for **worst moments**, not average ones.

---

### 3. **Engineering Fluency (Judgment, Not Coding)**

> You don’t need to design the circuit, but you must understand constraints.
> 

**Example:**

- Knowing why a thermal limit caps print speed
- Understanding why a firmware fix isn’t trivial post-launch
- Respecting manufacturing tolerances and QA cycles

**Translation:** PM earns engineering trust by making *realistic* calls.

---

### 4. **Market-Bound Product Thinking**

> Products are built for specific environments, not abstract users.
> 

**Example:**

- Sports bars vs fine-dining kitchens have different printer needs
- Digital artists value color fidelity over raw brightness
- Schools prioritize maintenance simplicity over top-end specs

**Translation:** PM starts with **context**, not features.

---

### 5. **Translation: Specs → Emotional Value** *(Differentiator)*

> Customers don’t buy specs. They buy outcomes.
> 

**Example:**

- “High lumens” → “You can see the game clearly even with lights on”
- “Fast print speed” → “Your kitchen keeps moving under pressure”

**Translation:** PM converts engineering capability into *felt benefit*.

---

### 6. **Operational Empathy** *(Differentiator)*

> You design for chaos, not ideal conditions.
> 

**Example:**

- Greasy kitchens, loud venues, stressed staff
- Equipment handled by non-technical users
- Failures happen at the worst possible time

**Translation:** PM respects frontline reality.

---

### 7. **Ecosystem & Partner Awareness** *(Differentiator)*

> Epson products live inside other people’s workflows.
> 

**Example:**

- Integrators installing projectors
- Artists designing immersive exhibits
- Restaurants relying on POS ecosystems

**Translation:** PM builds bridges, not just products.
